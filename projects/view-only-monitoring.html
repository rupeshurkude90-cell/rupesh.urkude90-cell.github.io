PROJECT 06 · CASE STUDY

Zoom View-Only Monitoring Portal

A centralized, read-only portal providing limited visibility into Zoom environment information.

Project overview

The portal provided a centralized, read-only view of selected Zoom environment information.

Business challenge

Confirm what information was difficult to access, which teams needed visibility and what operational decisions the portal supported.

Solution approach

A read-only interface for selected environment information. Do not describe it as real-time monitoring, alerting or full observability unless those capabilities are confirmed.
