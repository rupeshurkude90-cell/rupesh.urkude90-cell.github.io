Enterprise Hardware Recommendation Portal

Serverless Video Conferencing Hardware Standardization & Lifecycle Governance

Project Overview

The Enterprise Hardware Recommendation Portal is an internal web application designed to standardize video conferencing room hardware selection across 50 regional IT leads. Its primary purpose is to provide a centralized self-service catalog of approved vendor bundles while automatically preventing the procurement of End-of-Sale (EOS) or End-of-Life (EOL) equipment.

Business Problem

Regional IT leads previously relied on manual specification lookups and repetitive email consultations with central IT to select room hardware. This manual workflow caused administrative delays, inconsistent hardware configurations across global offices, missed vendor volume discounts, and costly accidental purchases of outdated EOS/EOL equipment.

Solution Implemented

A serverless web portal was built natively on the organization's existing Google Workspace Enterprise Standard platform, eliminating external server hosting and incremental licensing costs. The solution pairs a Google Apps Script web interface backed by Google Sheets/Drive storage with an automated scraper that tracks product lifecycle statuses and filters out EOS/EOL hardware.

Key Features

  • Standardized Hardware Catalog: Centralizes pre-approved enterprise video conferencing room bundles across approved vendors (Poly, Yealink, and Logitech).
  • Automated EOS/EOL Lifecycle Tracking: Uses an automated scraper and scheduled script triggers to monitor vendor product statuses and block or flag End-of-Sale and End-of-Life items.
  • Self-Service Bundle Selection: Guides 50 regional IT leads directly to validated, compliant room kits without requiring manual email approvals from central IT.
  • Serverless Zero-Infrastructure Hosting: Runs entirely within existing Google Workspace Enterprise entitlements, requiring zero external cloud VMs or third-party SaaS tools.
  • Enterprise Access Governance: Enforces domain-level security, execution quotas, and Context-Aware Access policies via the Google Workspace Admin Console.

Technology Stack

  • Google Apps Script: Serves as the core application runtime, backend logic engine, automated trigger scheduler, and Web App hosting layer.
  • Google Workspace Enterprise Standard: Provides the native serverless hosting environment, enterprise API quotas, and Cloud Identity authentication.
  • Google Sheets & Google Drive: Act as the native data storage layer for maintaining the approved hardware catalog and lifecycle status records.
  • Automated Web Scraping & Gmail Services: Used to monitor vendor EOS/EOL lifecycle updates and support native Workspace operational workflows.
  • Google Workspace Admin & Context-Aware Access: Governs internal domain access permissions and web app execution security.

My Role & Contribution

I led end-to-end requirement analysis, serverless solution architecture, and full-stack development of the Google Apps Script portal and automated EOS/EOL verification workflow. Additionally, I utilized AI assistance (Gemini) to validate financial modeling, structure the corporate ROI business case, and troubleshoot spreadsheet formula alignment for executive review.

Workflow

  • 1. Lifecycle Data Ingestion: An automated scraper periodically checks vendor product statuses and updates EOS/EOL flags in the central Google Sheets repository.
  • 2. User Authentication: A regional IT lead accesses the internal Google Apps Script Web App using their authenticated Google Workspace domain account.
  • 3. Catalog Filtering: The backend application reads the approved catalog (Poly, Yealink, Logitech) and automatically excludes hardware flagged as End-of-Life.
  • 4. Bundle Selection: The IT lead reviews the active, pre-approved room bundles and selects a standardized configuration suitable for their site.
  • 5. Compliant Procurement: The team procures the validated hardware bundle under enterprise volume-discount tiers without raising EOS/EOL purchase errors.

Challenges & Solutions

  • A key architectural challenge was delivering an enterprise-wide portal for 50 global sites without budget for dedicated servers or third-party licenses, which was resolved by leveraging native Google Apps Script and Workspace Enterprise quotas. During financial evaluation, formula double-counting between internal development labor and commercial revenue—along with multi-year licensing artifacts—was resolved by isolating one-time build costs from recurring operational savings.

Business Benefits

  • The portal eliminated manual email bottlenecks across 50 regional IT leads while standardizing room deployments across approved vendors to capture bulk purchasing discounts. It also prevented capital waste from accidental EOS/EOL hardware purchases while maintaining zero ongoing infrastructure or licensing overhead.
